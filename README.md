- Our VQCA approach mixes the power of Quantum Neural Networks(QNN) with the Quantum Cellular Automata (QCA), using Parameterized Quantum Circuits (PQC) and Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQA) to teach recursive patterns
- The objective of this research is to assess the learning capacity of parameterized recurrent quantum circuits in a unidimensional Cellular Automaton with a simple neighborhood function.
